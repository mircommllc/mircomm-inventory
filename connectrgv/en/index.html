Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost me?+

Nothing. You pay each company directly for your services, at the same price as if you signed up on your own.

If it’s free, how do you make money?+

The providers pay us a commission for each customer we connect. That’s why we never charge you.

Can I choose my electricity provider at my address?+

In most of the Valley, yes. Some rural areas are served by an electric co-op; if that’s your case, we’ll tell you how to sign up with them directly.

I’m moving tomorrow. Is there still time?+

Call us today. Many activations happen the same day, and if something takes longer, we’ll give you the real date up front.
